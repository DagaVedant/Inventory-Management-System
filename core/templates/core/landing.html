Know what is on the shelf and what is inside a build.

An electronics parts inventory built for a bench, not a warehouse. Parts do not get used up here. A project holds them, and when you tear it down most of them come back.

Create an account. A username and a password, it asks for nothing else.

Three numbers per part

  • Owned: everything you have, loose or inside a live build.
  • Held: sitting inside a project right now.
  • Available: owned minus held. The one you check before you start.

Short is what a build asked for and could not get. It lands on the shopping list.

What it does

  • Teardown: say what became of every part, returned, soldered in, or broken. Only the last two leave your inventory.
  • Reopen: a teardown is reversible in two clicks.
  • Shopping list: what live builds could not get, plus anything you flagged, totalled per part. Download it as CSV or plain text.
  • History: every quantity change logged with the balance it produced.
  • Duplicates: 10k and 10kΩ are one resistor. It spots them and merges them. 4.7k and 47k it leaves alone.
  • Paste import: a whole bin at once, one part per line.
  • Tags: filter by one, autocomplete so typos do not split a group, rename across every part.
