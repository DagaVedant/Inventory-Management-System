Know what is on the shelf and what is inside a build.

An electronics parts inventory built for a bench, not a warehouse. Parts do not get used up here. A project holds them, and when you tear it down most of them come back.

Create an account How it works

A username and a password. It asks for nothing else.

Three numbers per part

Owned
Everything you have, loose or inside a live build.
Held
Sitting inside a project right now.
Available
Owned minus held. The one you check before you start.

What it does

Teardown
Say what became of every part: returned, soldered in, or broken. Only the last two leave your inventory.
Reopen
A teardown is reversible in two clicks.
Shopping list
What live builds could not get, plus anything you flagged, totalled per part. Download it as CSV or plain text.
History
Every quantity change logged with the balance it produced.
Duplicates
10k and 10kΩ are one resistor. It spots them and merges them. 4.7k and 47k it leaves alone.
Paste import
A whole bin at once, one part per line.
Tags
Filter by one, autocomplete so typos do not split a group, rename across every part.
