How it works

A project borrows parts and holds them until you tear it down. Most come back. The ones soldered in or broken do not.

Three numbers

  • Owned: everything you have, loose or inside a live build.
  • Held: inside a project right now.
  • Available: owned minus held.

Teardown

  • Returned: back on the shelf.
  • Soldered in: gone for good.
  • Broken: gone for good.

Each line must add up to what the project was holding. Reopen undoes it.

Short

Asking for six when you own two gets you the two. The other four are recorded as short and land on the shopping list on the Bench.

Import format

name, quantity, value, package, tag, tag, ...
10k resistor, 180, 10k, through-hole, passive
DHT22, 4

Where things live

  • Bench: in progress, what to buy, what is running low.
  • Parts: everything you own. Search it, click a column to sort.
  • Projects: live builds and everything torn down.
  • Duplicates: 10k and 10kΩ are one part. 4.7k and 47k are not.
  • Tags: rename or remove a tag across every part at once.
  • Roster: benches whose owners chose to show them. Yours stays private until you say otherwise in settings.
